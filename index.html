Our story

Sri Geetha Fashions Hub is a neighbourhood fashion store for sarees, festive wear, kids' outfits and jewellery. We pick fabrics ourselves, price them fairly and help each customer find what suits the occasion and the budget.

Now you can also order online. Browse, choose, and send your order to us on WhatsApp. We confirm it personally, so you always know who you are dealing with.

How ordering works

From cart to doorstep in five steps

  1. Browse

    Search by category or name and open any product for details and sizes.

  2. Add to cart

    Pick your size and quantity. Your cart is saved on this device.

  3. Confirm

    Enter delivery details, choose how to pay and review the order.

  4. Send on WhatsApp

    One tap sends the full order to us. We reply to confirm stock and delivery.

  5. Pay and receive

    Pay by UPI, cash on delivery or at the store, then wait for your parcel.
